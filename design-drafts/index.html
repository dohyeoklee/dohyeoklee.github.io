Physics is not a dataset

Robots should not merely fit correlations in data. They should learn the structure that governs interaction.

Published
Sep 2026
Reading
6 min
Topics
robot learning, physics
Language

Language has a strange gift for robotics researchers: it arrives already compressed. Every sentence on the internet is someone's summary of the world. Physical interaction offers no such courtesy. A cup does not explain why it slips; it just slips, and the data we record is the residue of forces we never observe.1

1 Contact forces are rarely measured directly in manipulation datasets. Most of what a policy sees is pixels and joint angles.

But the slipping obeys something. Unlike language, interaction is governed by laws of nature, and laws are the one thing that holds outside the training distribution. A policy that learned the law instead of the pattern should fail more gracefully when the table changes.

Laws are the only part of the data that keeps working after you leave the lab.

§1 Two questions

Two questions guide my work. How should we represent physics so that a learning system can use it? And how do we move from predicting what happens next to understanding why?2 Dynamics models, novel view synthesis, and contact-aware control are three attempts at answers.

2 In DAP, the policy and a dynamics model correct each other at every flow step, so a candidate action is judged by the future it would produce.

None of these is finished. But each one moves the robot a little closer to knowing what its hands are doing, which is, in the end, the whole job.

Figure system. 논문 figure를 웹사이트와 같은 팔레트, 같은 모노 폰트로 그린 예시예요. π처럼 옅은 면(tint)과 진한 선(stroke)을 한 쌍으로 쓰고, 강조는 노랑 하나로만 해요. 테마를 Dark로 바꾸면 발표 슬라이드용 다크 버전도 확인할 수 있어요. 막대그래프 수치는 스타일 예시용 임의 값이에요.

Rules so far

  1. 세리프는 생각, 모노는 사실. 제목·본문·캡션은 세리프, 날짜·학회·라벨·figure 안 글자는 모노로 써요.
  2. 노랑은 한 페이지에 한 곳. 웹에서는 핵심 문장 하나, figure에서는 "우리 방법" 하나에만 써요.
  3. 구분은 선으로. 카드·그림자 대신 hairline으로 나눠요. 테두리는 강조할 한 요소에만 써요.
  4. figure 색은 순서가 고정. 회색(입력·베이스라인) → 노랑(우리) → 인디고 → 틸 → 코랄 → 플럼 → 세이지 순이에요.
  5. 사진은 흑백, 마우스를 올리면 컬러. 페이지 전체 톤을 지키면서 작업물은 생생하게 보여줘요.
